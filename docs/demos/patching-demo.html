Proposed Connect flow. Fictional data that lives only in this page; nothing is saved, and a reload resets it.
  1. Click a white (free) jack on the faceplate below. A small popup asks where the cable goes.
  2. Pick a recent target, or Choose far end…, to open the patching screen.
  3. Click a free jack on the right: the cable is made and both sides move to the next free jack. Press Enter to keep going.
  4. Switch to Range, set a count (or Shift-click the last near jack), click the far start, then Connect N.

What changes from today

  • No more one search box over every free port in the network: the far end is chosen by Site › Node › Rack › Equipment, starting at the near end's node.
  • You click real jacks on both faceplates, so you see exactly which position you use.
  • Each cable is saved at once and both sides advance; a range shows its numbered pairs on both faceplates before you confirm.
  • Port numbers continue past 24 inside a bank (Gi1/0/25 … Gi1/0/48), instead of starting again at 1.
  • The same Site › Node › Rack dropdowns would replace the long rack list in Add, Move and Duplicate.
