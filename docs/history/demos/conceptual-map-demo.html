DEMO
Proposed conceptual map for real data. Fictional data that lives only in this page. Card positions are kept in this browser only to show how a reload would look.
  1. The map opens on nodes, grouped by site: access buildings on the left, sub-mains in the middle, the AGG/BB rooms on the right. Each line is one pair of nodes with its cable count.
  2. Click a node to open its equipment in place. Drag a node to arrange the map; the position would be saved for everyone.
  3. Click a piece of equipment (or search for it) to Focus on it: only what is cabled to it, in large cards. +1 hop widens it; click a card to move the focus.

What changes from today

  • The map no longer opens on every piece of equipment in long rows. It opens on nodes, so a campus of hundreds of devices starts as a few dozen readable cards.
  • Columns follow cable distance from the AGG/BB rooms, not the role of each device, so a building's switches and panels stay together inside their node.
  • A line between two nodes is a summary of real cables (the number is the cable count). It never feeds a trace.
  • Focus shows one device and only what is cabled to it, with the access side on the left and the core side on the right. It is laid out automatically and is not saved.
  • Node positions you drag in the overview are saved on the server, shared with everyone, and kept after a reload, like node positions on the site screen in Physical. Reset arrangement returns to the automatic layout.
  • A trace still highlights in place: in the overview the nodes and lines on the route are marked; in Focus the route's cards are marked. The walk sheet stays in the results panel.
