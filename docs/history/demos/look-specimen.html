3. The type scale: one size per job

Six sizes (11 · 12 · 13 · 14 · 16 · 20), three weights (400 · 500 · 600). Letter-spacing follows IBM Carbon's productive set, which is tuned for Plex: a little looser when small, none from 16px up.

4. The colours, with their contrast

All ratios are computed in this page from the hex values (WCAG 2 formula). Text needs 4.5:1; lines, borders, icons and jack edges that carry meaning need 3:1.

Neutral greys: twelve steps, each with one job

Steel (racks, faceplates, HDFs only)

State colours

Network colours, in creation order

1–6 have the same brightness (white numbers ≥ 4.5:1 and ≥ 3:1 against the steel plate). 7–10 are a lighter row with dark numbers; 11–12 a deeper row. Every pair is at least 8 apart (ΔE OKLab × 100). Try the colour-vision menu: blues and purples merge, which is why a network is never shown by colour alone.
